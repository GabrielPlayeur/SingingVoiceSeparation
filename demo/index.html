Voice separation demo

Excerpts of two test tracks separated by the small stereo U-Net of this project (final model: width 32, 39 M parameters, trained 1000 epochs with data augmentation; median test SDR 5.89 dB over the 3 seeds, this is seed 42). For each track: the best and the worst 10 s excerpt, chosen by a fixed rule on the 1 s SDR scores (median over the excerpt), so a good passage does not hide a bad one. Passages without vocals are left out (their SDR only measures the noise floor). SDR is the BSSEval v4 median over the 1 s windows of the excerpt, as in the evaluation. The selection uses the ground truth: this describes the model, it is not a blind test.

These are the only two test tracks whose licence allows publishing them (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; the others are restricted). They are not representative: they are the two tracks out of 50 where the final model gains nothing over the 150-epoch baseline (−0.26 and −0.03 dB, against +0.99 dB median over the 50 tracks), and their whole-track SDR (2.9 and 3.5 dB) is below the median of the test set (5.9 dB). Read them as a hard case, not as a showcase.
