Voice separation demo

Excerpts of two test tracks separated by the small stereo U-Net of this project (checkpoint outputs/VoiceSeparation/fgvzjxua/checkpoints/baseline-stereo.ckpt). For each track: the best, a typical and the worst 10 s excerpt, chosen by a fixed rule on the 1 s SDR scores (median over the excerpt), so a good passage does not hide a bad one. Passages without vocals are left out (their SDR only measures the noise floor). SDR is the BSSEval v4 median over the 1 s windows of the excerpt, as in the evaluation. The selection uses the ground truth: this describes the model, it is not a blind test.
